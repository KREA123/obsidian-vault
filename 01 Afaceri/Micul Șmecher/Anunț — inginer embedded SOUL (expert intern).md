**ARTEMIS DIGITAL S.R.L.** (Bucharest) is building **SOUL**, a pocket AI companion device on the **ESP32-S3** in an aluminium enclosure. It has:
- a round 1.75″ AMOLED touch display (466 × 466), IMU, two microphones and a speaker, BLE and Wi-Fi, in a body of about 63 × 71 × 23 mm (the bench prototype uses a board with a 2.8″ screen);
- its own embedded OS (SoulOS) and a "living eyes" graphics engine;
- a cloud/MCP bridge that lets owners use their own AI assistant.

We are preparing a **PR București-Ilfov 1.1 "Proof of Concept"** research project (TRL 3 to TRL 5, 24 months).

**What you will do:**
- act as the project's technical and scientific lead: specifications, experimental protocols, test bench, result analysis;
- **validate, as the internal scientific expert, the TRL 3 lab report**. We re-run the tests together on the real board;

- mentor one firmware engineer and one student.

- **at least 5 years of documented experience** in embedded/firmware on microcontrollers (ESP32/ESP-IDF, STM32, nRF or similar): C/C++, RTOS (FreeRTOS/Zephyr), I²C/SPI/I²S, BLE/Wi-Fi;
