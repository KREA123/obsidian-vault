# MundiShop: what 15 Meta orders a day would realistically take (as of 25.09.2026)

Read-only: nothing changed in any account, no credentials read or quoted. Figures come from the vault plus 2025–2026 web benchmarks (sources at the end).

## Bottom line
- **Where you are:** Meta brings about 1.3 orders a day (40 in 30 days, at 188 lei per order). 15 a day is about 11× that. It is also double the vault's own Q4 plan, which expects 6–8 a day from Meta and 12–15 a day for the whole store.
- **The equation that decides it:** cost per order = cost per click ÷ the share of clicks that buy.
  - With the old broad catalog (0.85 lei per click, 0.65% of clicks buying), 15 a day costs about 1,950 lei a day and loses about 1,050 lei a day, roughly 32k lei a month.
  - With Teme (about 3 lei per click): about 7,200 lei a day. Not workable.
- **What break-even needs:** cost per order of about 60 lei or less. That requires **cost per click of about 0.9 lei or less AND 1.4–1.5% or more of clicks buying, at the same time, under Q4 ad prices.** That is 2–2.5× today's conversion. It is the exceptional case, not the base case.
- **What is plausible:** 15 orders on individual peak days on 1–18 December, bought at a loss (about 1,000–1,400 lei a day at about 90 lei per order). Realistic and profitable: 5–8 Meta orders a day in December, if the checkout, offer and creative fixes land in October.
- **Biggest levers:** one broad catalog instead of 7 split ad sets (cost per click about 3.5× lower); checkout with cash on delivery (COD) first. The root cause ads can't fix is price: 92% of products sit above market.

## The model
Orders per day = budget × click-through rate (CTR) × click-to-purchase rate (CVR) ÷ (cost per 1,000 impressions (CPM) ÷ 1,000). Put simply, orders = budget ÷ cost per order. Cost per click (CPC) = CPM ÷ (1,000 × CTR).

Example: at CPM 25 lei, 1,000 lei buys 40k impressions → 1,000 clicks at 2.5% CTR → 7 orders at 0.7% CVR.

| 0.60 | 1,800 (120) | 1,286 (86) | 900 (60) | 643 (43) | 450 (30) |

| **3.00 (Teme now)** | 9,000 (600) | 6,429 (429) | 4,500 (300) | 3,214 (214) | 2,250 (150) |

To reach break-even (cost per order of about 60 lei or less) you need at least 1.0% of clicks buying at 0.60 lei per click, at least 1.4% at 0.85 lei, or about 2% at 1.0–1.3 lei.

Two notes on the table:
- **Scenario 2b is my estimate.**
  - Accessories convert 2.6× better and were 53% of units in the old campaign. If they were about 45% of its orders, a sets-only catalog converts at about 72% of the old rate.
  - Removing accessories protected margin but cut conversion: part of the historical 0.54–0.7% is gone.
- **Scenarios 4–6 need 1,000–1,300 Meta clicks a day.**
  - That is 1.6–2× today's total traffic (about 620 sessions a day), and far above September's ceiling: above about 150–180 lei a day, the catalog's cost per order broke.

## The highest-impact levers
1. **One broad catalog / Advantage+ ad set, after the 01.10 verdict.**
   - This cuts cost per click from about 3.09 to 0.85–1.0 lei, roughly a 3–3.5× effect on cost per order. It is the largest move available.
   - Proven here: 41 purchases at 126 lei each, CTR 2.48%.
   - Meta reports −12% median cost per result from merging ad sets and −7.2% from Advantage+ audience (from the vault's Meta note, marked unverified). Gender is locked in 6 of the 7 Teme ad sets today.
   - Fill it with products that convert: sets under 200 lei (6× the conversion of those over 200), bundles, and the 73 sets that can sit at market price.
   - **Trade-off:** sets priced 150–250 lei can only carry about 37–50 lei per order in ads. This lever raises order count more than profit.
2. **Checkout: cash on delivery shown first and preselected.** Also show the shipping cost on the product page and in the cart, and test Google Pay.
   - Raising completion from 47–50% to about 60% adds 20–30% to conversion, which cuts cost per order by 17–23%, at almost no cost.
   - Shopify lets you reorder non-card payment methods with a payment-customization app (Settings › Payments › Payment customizations). The card restriction applies only in the US and Canada.
3. **Offer and price: this is the root cause.**
   - 92% of the catalog is priced above market.
   - On Romanian Black Friday (7.11.2025) eMAG sold LEGO at about −40% (City Tram Station 194 lei instead of 329).
   - What moves conversion for good: bundles that can't be price-compared, sets under 200 lei, and a small market-price test on the 73-set list.
   - Without this, click-to-purchase stays stuck near 1%.
4. **Trust and stock.**
   - Get from 0 reviews to at least 5–10 on the top products. The Spiegel Research Center found 5 reviews raise purchase likelihood 270% compared with none, more so on expensive items. Across the whole store, expect more like +10–20%.
   - Fix the stock sync: 328 products show 0 stock.
   - Align the delivery promise: the site says 24–72h, the ads say 24–48h.
5. **More creative, in the right formats.**
   - Make 9:16 build and unboxing videos, add video to the catalog ads, and run 1–2 creator partnership ads.
   - Meta's own numbers: partnership ads −19% cost per order and +13% CTR; catalog video +20% conversions per leu; ad sets with at least 20% vertical video with sound −7% cost per order (April 2026 data).
   - I could not confirm the vault's "−34.5%" figure.
   - Stacked realistically: about −15–25% cost per order. Static ads get almost no delivery today (about 88% of spend goes to catalog ads).

Secondary: page speed (Deloitte: 0.1s faster on mobile → +8.4% retail conversions; unmeasured here, 96% mobile traffic). A free-shipping threshold (vendor claims +10–30%) is blocked by Andu's rule and would eat about 40% of contribution.

So 15 a day, sustained, is not on the realistic path before 2027. At best it shows up for a few December days, and at a loss unless about 1.4% of clicks buy.

## Constraints to flag
- **VAT threshold:** 15 a day × 253 lei × about 85 days ≈ 323k lei in Q4 alone. With revenue earlier in the year, that is close to the 395k lei threshold. Crossing it cuts margin by about 17%, and break-even cost per order drops to about 50 lei. Check with the accountant first.
- **Pace:** at 1–2 orders a day a verdict needs 7–14 days, so with one change per week only a few levers land before December. Order: ad structure → checkout → reviews and stock → creative.
- **Operations:** about 450 orders a month needs the Clever Toys stock sync fixed and refused COD parcels (about 8–12%) priced in.
- **Cost caps are not a shortcut:** the 20 lei cap (24–25.09) and the 45 lei cost target (31.08) both stopped delivery.
