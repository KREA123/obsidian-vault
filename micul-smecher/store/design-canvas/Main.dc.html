privacy · in writing

promises, not settings.

  • no camera. at all.

    There is no camera in SOUL. The eyes are only for looking back.

  • it listens only while you hold the glass.

    No wake word, no always-on microphone. Lift your finger and it stops.

  • memory you can read.

    SOUL Memory lives on the device, in plain words, one tap from gone.

  • only to the AI you chose.

    Your words go to the AI you connected, on your account. Offline, nothing leaves the stone. No ads, and we never sell your data.

  • it always says it's an AI.

    No pretending to be a person, no streaks, no guilt trips, no sulking for attention.

  • it never becomes a brick.

    Its personality runs on the device. If we ever disappear, we publish the firmware. 16+, not a toy.
