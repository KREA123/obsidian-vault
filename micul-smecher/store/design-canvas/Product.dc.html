which AI does it work with?+

can I buy it now?+

Not yet. SOUL has Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, so it must pass CE testing (the EU Radio Equipment Directive) at an accredited lab before anyone can sell it. It isn't CE-certified yet. Until then there is only a waitlist: no payment, no pre-orders, no deposits.
