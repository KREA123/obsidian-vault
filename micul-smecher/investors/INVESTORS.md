### 1.3 Solo non-technical founder risk: how to fix it
Investors read "solo + non-technical + hardware" as *execution risk on the hardest part*. Fixes, strongest first [E]:
1. **Technical co-founder (firmware + electronics)**, 10–25 % equity, 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff, full-time from the close of the proof round. Profile: shipped an ESP32/nRF consumer or IoT product through CE; knows BLE, power, RF in metal enclosures (the #1 SOUL risk, 10 §4). Where: Garmin Cluj, Bosch/Continental RO, Politehnica București (ETTI) and Cluj labs, Romanian maker scene (xiaozhi/ESP32 contributors on GitHub), Innovation Labs alumni [K]. Pitch to them: the code base already exists (firmware + tests + AI), they inherit a running project, not a slide.
2. **Advisors (0.25–1 % each, 2-year vesting):** (a) a hardware operations person who has taken a product from EVT to PVT in Shenzhen; (b) an industrial designer/CMF person (aluminium, anodising); (c) an AI/agent product person close to the Claude/MCP ecosystem; (d) a Romanian angel with a known exit (signal for local funds).
3. **Contract engineering with milestone pay** as a bridge: a Romanian or Polish design house for PCBA/RF (€15–30k PCBA design, 09) so the pilot does not wait for the hire.
4. **Show founder-market fit:** Andu's edge is brand, e-commerce and paid acquisition (MundiShop), exactly what kills most hardware start-ups (Friend spent $1M on ads for ~3k units [V-vault 11]). Put the MundiShop numbers in the deck.
5. **Full-time commitment** from the first institutional cheque; say how MundiShop/Expandly are run without him.

### 1.4 SAFE vs equity in Romania
- **A SAFE is a US contract written for Delaware C-corps** [K]: no interest, no maturity, converts at the next priced round [V-s SeedBlink/Wikipedia]. A Romanian SRL can sign a SAFE-style contract, but conversion still needs a shareholders' decision to raise share capital and an ONRC filing; the right to shares is contractual, so investors want a well-drafted local-law instrument [K/E]. SeedBlink publishes explainers on both SAFEs and **CLAs (convertible loan agreements)** for European start-ups [V-s], the common CEE instrument.
- **Recommendation [E]:**
  - **Angel proof round:** a **convertible loan agreement under Romanian law** (or a Romanian-law "SAFE" adapted by a start-up lawyer): valuation cap €2–3M, 15–20 % discount, 0–4 % interest, 24-month maturity with conversion (not repayment) at maturity. One document for all angels. Budget €2–5k legal.
  - **Pooling:** many small angels → one line on the cap table via a SeedBlink vehicle ("one cap table line instead of multiple individual angels", €0 SPV set-up [V]) or a TechAngels syndicate.
  - **Priced pre-seed (€1M+):** equity, with a shareholders' agreement (tag/drag, ROFR, vesting for founders, leaver clauses). Consider converting SRL → **SA** or a holding when the cap table grows [K].
  - **YC/US funds** require a Delaware (or similar) parent and a "flip" of the SRL under it [K]; budget $5–15k and only do it if a US cheque is on the table.
  - **ESOP:** SRL share options are clumsy; use a **virtual/phantom ESOP** until an SA/holding exists [K].
  - New rules: SRLs founded from 2026 need **RON 500 minimum capital**, and transfers of a controlling stake carry new ANAF formalities (Law 239/2025) [V-s CMS/KleaLegal summaries].
