What we collect

  • your email address;
  • the language you want emails in, and the colour you picked (optional);

To email you when reservations for SOUL open, and to send the odd update about the product before that. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email or by writing to us. Withdrawing doesn't affect what we did before.

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. If reservations have not opened 24 months after you signed up, we delete your entry.

The store and its customer list run on Shopify (Shopify International Limited, Dublin, Ireland), which acts as our processor and also sends the emails. On this preview page the form sends nothing: a sign-up stays only in your own browser.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to its use, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Write to office@krea.ro; we answer within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Romanian data protection authority, ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro).

SOUL has no camera. SOUL Memory lives on the device. If you connect an AI (your own Claude or ChatGPT), what you say goes to that provider under your own account and their terms. A full privacy notice for the device will be published before sales open.
