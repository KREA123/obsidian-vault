questions

good questions.

Can I buy it now?
Not yet. SOUL has Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, so it must pass CE testing (the EU Radio Equipment Directive) at an accredited lab before anyone can sell it. It isn't CE-certified yet. Until then there is only a waitlist: no payment, no pre-orders, no deposits.
Do I need a subscription?
Not to us. SOUL is alive offline, with no account. For AI, you connect your own Claude or ChatGPT, on your own account or API key, and pay them directly. We never sell or include AI.
Which AI does it work with?
Claude: your own Anthropic account or API key, plus a connector for the Claude app. ChatGPT: your own OpenAI API key works now; “Sign in with ChatGPT” is pending OpenAI's approval. Or no AI at all. SOUL is in development, and these connections are being built.
What is SOUL Memory?
The things SOUL remembers about you: names, plans, preferences. It lives on the device, in plain words you can read and delete. When you talk, it goes along to whichever AI you connected, so switching AIs doesn't mean starting over.
Does it listen all the time? Does it have a camera?
No and no. It listens only while your finger is on the glass. There is no wake word and no camera.
Can I choose its eyes?
No, and that's the fun. You choose the colour of the body. The eyes are decided once, at first power-on, from the chip's unique ID: one of 120 designs, some much rarer than others. The odds are published above.
Does it work away from home?
Yes. Offline it's always alive. For AI it needs Wi-Fi, and it can roam on your phone's hotspot.
How long does the battery last?
We'll publish measured battery life before we ask anyone to pay. It charges in OU, its egg.
Is it a toy?
No. SOUL is made for adults and teens 16 and over. It contains small parts and a lithium battery.
Who makes it?
ARTEMIS DIGITAL S.R.L., in Bucharest, Romania. Designed in Romania. We'll name our manufacturing partners before we ask anyone to pay.
