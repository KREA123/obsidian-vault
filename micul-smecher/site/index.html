Questions

Good questions.

When will it ship?
Founders Desk Edition: estimated spring 2027. Voice Edition: estimated autumn 2027. If we're late, you can cancel for a full refund at any time before it ships. After delivery you have the EU 14-day right of withdrawal and a 2-year legal guarantee.
Do I need a subscription?
No. SOUL is alive with no internet and no account, forever. The Voice Edition includes 6 months of voice AI. After that, an optional plan keeps the voice features on. Without it, your SOUL stays a full offline companion.
Which AI does it use?
None, if you like: SOUL works with no AI and no account. Or connect your own Claude or ChatGPT (in development). For voice: the SOUL voice service (Claude by default) or your own Anthropic or OpenAI API key. “Works with Claude” uses the Hardware Buddy developer API of the Claude desktop apps, so the stone can show what Claude Code and Claude Cowork are doing and let you approve their requests.
Does it need my phone or computer?
Not to be alive. To work with Claude, it pairs over Bluetooth with the Claude desktop app on your Mac or Windows PC.
How long does the battery last?
The Founders Desk Edition has no battery: it runs on USB-C. The Voice Edition has a small, user-replaceable battery that charges over USB-C. We'll publish measured battery life before we ask for full payment.
Is it a toy?
No. SOUL is made for adults and teens 16 and over. It contains small parts and, in the Voice Edition, a lithium battery. Keep it away from young children.
What happens to my voice and my data?
It listens only while your finger is on the stone. Voice requests go to servers in the EU and to the AI you chose, and nowhere else. Its memory is a list you can read and delete. No camera, no ads, and we never sell your data.
Can I choose its eye colour?
No, and that's the point. Each stone is born with its own colour and personality, decided by its chip. Every colour has the same features: rarity changes only the colour.
What if your company disappears?
Your SOUL keeps living, because its personality runs on the device. We'll also publish the firmware, so it never becomes a brick.
Where is it made?
Designed in Bucharest, Romania. We'll name our manufacturing partner before we ask for full payment.
