Questions

Good questions.

When will it ship?
SOUL ships only after it passes CE radio testing: estimated spring 2027. After delivery you have the EU 14-day right of withdrawal and a 2-year legal guarantee.
Is it CE certified?
Not yet, and that's why SOUL waits. SOUL has Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, so it must pass CE radio testing (the EU Radio Equipment Directive) at an accredited lab before anyone can sell it. We won't ship a single unit before that.
Do I need a subscription?
No. SOUL is alive with no internet and no account, forever. SoulOS, the keyboard, notes, reminders, alarms and focus all work offline. For AI, you connect the Claude or ChatGPT you already have, or your own API key.
Which AI does it use?
The one you choose, in its AI modes: no AI at all, your Claude, your ChatGPT, or your own Anthropic or OpenAI API key. You switch from its Control Center. Claude and ChatGPT connections are in development. “Works with Claude” uses the Hardware Buddy developer API of the Claude desktop apps, so the stone can show what Claude Code and Claude Cowork are doing and let you approve their requests.
What happens to my voice and my data?
It listens only while your finger is on the stone. Close the lid of its egg, and the microphone is cut in hardware. With no AI, nothing leaves the stone. With AI, your request goes only to the AI you chose. Our own servers are in the EU. Its memory is a list you can read and delete. No camera, no ads, and we never sell your data.
How long does the battery last?
SOUL has a small rechargeable lithium battery inside. It charges from the five gold contacts on its bottom: you set it in OU, its egg, and that's it. No cable to plug into the stone. We'll publish measured battery life before we ask for full payment.
What is it made of?
A body of aluminium 6061, bead-blasted and anodised, in Silver, Graphite, Midnight, Ember or Champagne. The front is black glass over a round 2.8″ IPS screen, 480 × 480 pixels. The speaker is a fine slit in the side seam. Underneath is a matching polymer plate, because metal blocks radio, with a small flat foot and the charging contacts. Size M, about 90 × 103 × 31 mm. The first bodies are CNC-machined from solid aluminium.
Does it need my phone or computer?
Not to be alive, and not to type: it has its own keyboard. To reach your Claude or ChatGPT it uses Wi-Fi. To work with Claude Code and Cowork, it pairs over Bluetooth with the Claude desktop app on your Mac or Windows PC.
Can I choose its eye colour?
You choose the colour of its body, not of its eyes. Each stone is born with its own eye colour and personality, decided by its chip. Every colour has the same features: rarity changes only the colour.
Is it a toy?
No. SOUL is made for adults and teens 16 and over. It contains small parts and a lithium battery. Keep it away from young children.
What if your company disappears?
Your SOUL keeps living, because its personality runs on the device. We'll also publish the firmware, so it never becomes a brick.
Where is it made?
Designed in Bucharest, Romania. We'll name our manufacturing partners before we ask for full payment.
