questions

good questions.

When will it ship?
Only after it passes CE radio testing: estimated spring 2027. Right now there is only a waitlist. Nothing is charged and there are no pre-orders. After delivery you have the EU 14-day right of withdrawal and a 2-year legal guarantee.
Do I need a subscription?
No. SOUL is alive with no internet and no account, forever. SoulOS, the keyboard, notes, reminders, alarms and focus all work offline. For AI, you connect the Claude or ChatGPT you already have, or your own API key.
Can I choose its eyes?
No, and that's the fun. You choose the colour of the body. The eyes are decided once, at first power-on, from the chip's unique ID: one of 120 designs, some much rarer than others. Rarity changes only the look. Every SOUL does exactly the same things.
Which AI does it use?
The one you choose: no AI at all, your Claude, your ChatGPT, or your own Anthropic or OpenAI API key. You switch from its Control Center. Claude and ChatGPT connections are in development. “Works with Claude” uses the Hardware Buddy developer API of the Claude desktop apps, so SOUL can show what Claude Code and Claude Cowork are doing and let you approve their requests.
What happens to my voice and my data?
It listens only while your finger is on the glass. Close the lid of its egg and the microphone is cut in hardware. With no AI, nothing leaves the stone. With AI, your request goes only to the AI you chose. Our own servers are in the EU. No camera, no ads, and we never sell your data.
How long does the battery last?
It has a small rechargeable lithium battery and charges in OU, its egg, through five gold contacts in its foot. We'll publish measured battery life before we ask anyone to pay.
Is it CE certified?
Not yet, and that's why SOUL waits. It has Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, so it must pass CE radio testing (the EU Radio Equipment Directive) at an accredited lab before anyone can sell it. We won't ship a single unit before that.
Is it a toy?
No. SOUL is made for adults and teens 16 and over. It contains small parts and a lithium battery.
What if your company disappears?
Your SOUL keeps living, because its personality runs on the device. We'll also publish the firmware, so it never becomes a brick.
Where is it made?
Designed in Romania. We'll name our manufacturing partners before we ask anyone to pay.
