## Claude usage policy (Anthropic AUP, effective 2025-09-15) [V]
- Minors = under 18; products serving minors must follow extra guidelines (age verification, moderation, monitoring, disclosure) — [support article 2026-03-16](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9307344-responsible-use-of-anthropic-s-models-guidelines-for-organizations-serving-minors).
- "All consumer-facing chatbots … must disclose to users that they are interacting with AI rather than a human."
- Prohibited: erotic chats; subliminal/manipulative techniques; exploiting age/disability/social situation; promoting self-harm. Companionship itself is not banned.
- Claude.ai consumer terms 18+. Anthropic discourages emotional dependency; ThroughLine crisis banners in 170+ countries.
→ Launch 18+ (or 16+ with care), say "AI" on box and first boot, no romantic mode, no guilt-trip retention, crisis routing, keep the model layer switchable.
