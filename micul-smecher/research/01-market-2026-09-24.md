## Top 10 insights
1. Price gap at **€79–129** between mass hits (€45–60) and design objects ($139–599). Starboy added a $139 tier. Target €89 early bird / €119–129 retail; numbered metal editions €199–299, small runs.
2. Price for pairs: pledges ≈ 2× unit price → "friend pair" hero reward (~€169).
3. Rarity drives collecting (Starboy 400+ eye looks, Eiliko mystery box, Dasai drops). Plan €15–30 accessory shells; Ropet 30–40% accessory repeat rate.
4. Buyer = women 20–30s, not gadget fans. Market as fashion + emotion, not as ESP32/AI.
5. "No app, no cloud" earned Starboy its press; Moxie died when its cloud shut down → "it can never be switched off by a server".
6. No Kickstarter from Romania → Indiegogo/Gamefound, a PL/GR/SI company, or Starboy-style direct batch preorders on Shopify.
7. Pre-launch list: 1,000 backers ≈ 30K emails ≈ $30–90K ads; cheaper: ~2,000 $1–5 VIP deposits → 500–1,000 backers ≈ €100–200K.
8. Realistic campaign: **€250–650K** (Mirumi $478K, Eiliko $600K, Sweekar $644K). Low goal (€10–20K) → "funded in minutes" PR.
9. Distribution beyond TikTok Shop; Starboy sold out with 19K TikTok followers via X + press.
10. BOM €20–35 → ~70% GM at €119. >75% ship late → promise 3 months after internal plan; CE/RED + GPSR ready before shipping.
