# EU / Romania compliance brief (raw, 2026-09-24) — not legal advice

Tags: [V] checked against official text · [S] secondary · [K] legal text from knowledge (EUR-Lex was bot-blocked) · [E] estimate.

## Three findings that change the plan
1. **Waveshare boards use a bare ESP32-S3R8 chip + onboard antenna, not a WROOM module** → no module radio certificate to reuse; full radio testing. With an ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 on our own PCB we can reuse part of Espressif's CE radio evidence [S].
2. **Battery removability (Battery Reg. 2023/1542 Art. 11) applies per unit placed on the market from 18 Feb 2027** — no postponement (Commission guidelines C(2026) 5032, 14 Jul 2026) [V]. A 2026 preorder delivered after that date counts as placed after it [K/E]. Cheapest: make the battery user-replaceable now.
3. **A wearable with mic/camera/IMU falls under RED Art. 3(3)(e) cybersecurity even offline** → EN 18031-2 [S]. A desk-only, no-internet, non-toy unit is probably outside 2022/30 [E].

## Battery Regulation
- "Readily removable": commercially available tools; product-specific tools only if included free; no proprietary tools, heat or solvents [V]. Instructions, works with compatible batteries, no parts-pairing, spares for 5 years [V].
- 2026 delegated act (ST 12056/26) adds a wearables derogation only where safety/durability/water resistance requires it **and** the device is too small or needs a compact sealed enclosure; publication in OJ unconfirmed [V/E]. A 400–500 mAh pouch in a 40–50 mm case is hard to argue as "too small" [E].
- Design: screwed back (Phillips/Torx), JST connector, foam/bracket not glue, protection inside the pack, no battery authentication [E].
- Battery CE + DoC (Module A) since 18 Aug 2024; crossed-bin symbol since 18 Aug 2025; full label/QR likely late 2027–2028 [S]. Importing bare cells = battery importer: get cell maker's DoC + UN38.3 summary [K/E]. EPR registration in every EU country of first sale [S]. Due diligence only ≥ €40M turnover [S].

## RED 2014/53/EU
- Standards: EN 300 328 (2.4 GHz), EN 301 489-1/-17 (EMC), EN IEC 62368-1 (safety; cells IEC 62133-2), EN 62479 / EN 62311 (RF exposure; body-worn EN 50663 or SAR EN 50566). Manual must list bands + max power [K/E].
- Self-declaration (Module A) allowed with harmonised standards; finished-product EMC + radiated tests always needed; no small-series exemption for consumer presales [K/S].
- Cyber (Delegated Reg. 2022/30, since 1 Aug 2025): 3.3(d) internet-connected (directly or via other equipment); 3.3(e) privacy for internet-connected **or** toy / childcare / **wearable**. EN 18031-1/-2/-3 harmonised with restrictions → notified body if a restriction is hit [S].
- Stay on self-declaration: unique per-device credentials, signed OTA, BLE LE Secure Connections, secure boot + flash encryption, debug locked in production [E].
- **CRA:** vulnerability/incident reporting since 11 Sep 2026; full obligations 11 Dec 2027 (incl. BLE); 2022/30 to be repealed then [S/K].
- Cost: Shenzhen RED ~US$2.8k + safety ~$1.8k; EN 18031 $6–9.5k; EU €2.5–8k (LCAS) to €10–20k; working range PL/RO €5–12k, DE €8–20k, SAR +€3–10k, retests +20–40%. 4–6 weeks best case, 8–12 with fixes [S/E].

## GPSR 2023/988
Risk analysis + technical file 10 years; type/batch/serial on product; name, postal + e-mail address; RO-language instructions; complaints channel; accidents via Safety Business Gateway; online listing must show manufacturer, picture, identifier, warnings (Art. 19). 2-year legal guarantee [K/S].

## Toys
Toy = designed/intended for play by children under 14; dual-purpose counts (teddy key-ring example). "14+ collectors" exclusion only if clearly stated; foreseeable use decides. Stay outside: adult desk/tech gadget marketing, "Not a toy. Not intended for children under 14", no child play claims. New Toy Safety Regulation 2025/2509 applies from 1 Aug 2030 [S/V].

## Romania
- ANPM merged into **ANMAP** (2025). EEE producer register: 500 lei, collective-scheme contract, ≤ 30 working days; annual 100 lei. Battery producer register: 500 lei, ≤ 15 working days [V].
- AFM monthly declarations by the 25th; transfer targets to a collective organisation. Timbru verde e.g. Environ cat. 6 ≤ 0.2 kg: 0.15 lei/unit [V/S].
- Year-1 cost ≈ €200 fees + a few hundred €/yr schemes + €30–100/month reporting [E].
- Selling to other EU countries: register + authorised representative per country (DE stiftung ear, FR ADEME) ≈ €1–3k per country per year [V/S/E]. **Edition 0: sell in Romania only, or via EU distributors.**

## Shipping
UN38.3 summary from cell supplier (custom pack: $300–930; IEC 62133-2 $1–6k). 1.85 Wh → **UN3481, PI 967 Section II**; no lithium mark for ≤ 2 packages × ≤ 2 batteries. DHL/UPS/FedEx accept; RO domestic couriers generally accept devices with battery installed by road; Deutsche Post / Poste Italiane refuse lithium in post → use express couriers [V/S].

## Consumer law (presale / crowdfunding)
14-day withdrawal from receipt; refund within 14 days; accept cancellation before delivery; **withdrawal button required from 19 Jun 2026** (Directive 2023/2673); delivery ≤ 30 days unless an estimated ship month is agreed; crowdfunding rewards from a business = distance sale [K/S].
Suggested wording: "Pre-order – Edition 0 (hand-assembled, limited to 100 units). Full price €X charged today. Estimated shipping: [Month Year]. If we have not shipped by [date + 30 days], you may cancel for a full refund at any time. You may also cancel before shipping, and you have a 14-day right of withdrawal from the day you receive the device. 2-year legal guarantee of conformity applies."

## Privacy / AI Act
On-device only → manufacturer not a GDPR controller; cloud/app/telemetry → controller. Face *detection* is minimal risk; don't market "reads your emotions" (emotion recognition outside work/school is high-risk from 2 Dec 2027; Art. 50(3) transparency). Conversational AI → **Art. 50(1) disclosure from 2 Aug 2026** [S/E].

## Checklist & budget
**(a) Edition 0 (50–100 units): ≈ €5–12k** (China lab, Romania only) to **€15–30k** (EU lab + DE/FR); **10–14 weeks** from design freeze. Items: hardware choice (WROOM vs Waveshare), replaceable battery + supplier docs, RED tests, cyber scope memo / EN 18031, RoHS/REACH declarations, technical file + DoC + labels + RO/EN manual, ANMAP + schemes + AFM, presale terms + withdrawal button + privacy notice, liability insurance, PI 967 shipping.
**(b) 1,000+ units: ≈ €30–80k, 4–6 months** (full RED at EU lab, EN 18031 ± notified body, SAR if wearable, Art. 11 evidence + spares, CRA readiness if after 11 Dec 2027, battery label/QR, custom pack tests, EPR in 3–5 countries, contingency, insurance/legal).
