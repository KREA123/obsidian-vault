## 2. Why people bond: the science, with numbers
- **Baby schema** (Lorenz): big eyes, high forehead, round face, small nose/mouth [V]. Glocker 2009 manipulated these parameters: 122 raters found high-schema faces cuter and more caretaking-worthy, with more nucleus-accumbens activity [V/V-s]. Gould: Mickey's head and eyes grew over the decades [V]. Chibi: head = ⅓–½ of height [V].
- **Eye size**: **+15 %** eye-to-face size made faces cuter across *all* expressions, in adults and infants alike, with no reversal up to +15 % [V Frontiers 2021].
- **Kawaii ≠ baby care.** It is a social *approach* emotion; baby schema is only one trigger (Nittono 2016) [V-s]. Cute things are hyper-mentalised and trigger play and sociality (Sherman & Haidt 2011) [V-s], and they make people more careful (Sherman 2009) [V].
- **Curves win**: sharp contours read as threat; people prefer curved objects (Bar & Neta 2006) [V-s].
- **Uncanny valley**: eyes are the strongest trigger (removing the eyes was eerier than removing the nose); mixing realism levels is uncanny; neotenous cartoon features remove it; **motion amplifies it** [V].
- **Screen faces** (157 robots × 76 features): the faces rated least friendly and least trusted were those **without a mouth, without pupils, or with eyelids**; "creepy", "surveillance", fit for security jobs. An angry-lidded face (Gongzi) read as "menacing" to 17/50 people. Over ⅓ of screen faces have a black background; circular eyes dominate [V IEEE].
- **Animacy from motion**: in Heider–Simmel (1944), 32 of 34 viewers described moving triangles as people [V-s]. Cues: self-propulsion, sudden speed or direction change, contingency; causal "launch" perception breaks beyond **140 ms** [V].
- **Blinks**: adults ~17/min (intervals 2–10 s), 4–5/min when reading; a blink lasts 100–150 ms; **infants 1–2/min** [V].
- **Gaze** (Argyle): listeners look at the speaker 75 % of the time, speakers at listeners 40 %; mutual gaze ~25 %; a glance lasts ~3 s, mutual gaze ~1 s [V-s].
- **Animation**: squash and stretch *at constant volume*, anticipation, slow-in/out, arcs; "appeal = charisma" [V].
- **Self-righting**: centre of mass below the centre of the base's curvature, so it "wobbles… and seeks upright" (okiagari-koboshi, the Daruma doll: "fall seven times, get up eight") [V].
- **Tsukumogami**: in Japanese folklore, tools acquire a spirit and grow eyes; mischievous, not evil [V]. This is exactly SOUL's story: *an object that got a soul*.

## 3. Kawaii vs cool for 18–35: how to be șmecher, not childish
**The market is adult now:** ~40 % of Europeans bought toys for themselves or another adult in 2025; collectibles grew +32 % to ~19 % of global toy $ [V Circana]; adults (kidults) are ~28 % of global toy sales [V-s]; Labubu buyers 18+ added ~$800M to US toys in 2024 [V-s]; Fuzozo and Ropet sell mostly to young women [V]. **But adult buyers pay a premium for *status + edge*, not sweetness.**

**Definition of cool:** autonomy that is *appropriate*: a small or occasional divergence from a norm that feels unnecessary (Warren & Campbell 2014) [V-s]. Cool brands are extraordinary, aesthetic, energetic, high-status, rebellious, original, authentic, subcultural, iconic, popular (Warren et al. 2019) [V-s].

**Seven moves that turn cute into cool [E, synthesised from §1–2]:**
1. **Cute lives in the software, cool in the hardware.** The object is serious, dense, glossy and permanent; the eyes are warm and playful and can be tuned. This is the "kuudere" archetype, cool outside and warm inside [K], and EVE's visor. The loved pebble already does it: the glass sits high like a helmet visor (forehead:chin 1:1.9), while the eyes inside it sit low like a baby's.
2. **Attitude comes from the lids, not from props.** A level, half-lowered lid reads unimpressed and calm, which is cool. The 8–10° V reads mischief/șmecher. Past ~15° it reads angry or menacing (Gongzi). Round, wide-open eyes are for moments only.
3. **Bounded autonomy.** It looks away sometimes, keeps its own sleep rhythm, and doesn't beg. No sulking or guilt (Fuzozo users disliked the sulking; Tamagotchi death is a kid mechanic). It has opinions within limits: that is Warren's "appropriate divergence".
4. **One flaw or edge**: Labubu's teeth, Kuromi's skull, KAWS's X-eyes, Aggretsuko's metal. For SOUL: the asymmetric smug lid plus a dry voice. Never hearts, tears, blush or confetti.
5. **Adult material tells** (08): density ≥ 1.8 g/cm³, gloss pearl or onyx, a single crisp edge. Kid tells to avoid: fur, ears, arms, wheels, decals, primary colours (Emo, Eilik and Loona all read as kids' gadgets).
6. **Subculture and drops.** Colourways and collabs like Bearbrick or Pop Mart, but the personality is never sold blind-box (V-05).
7. **Melancholy and calm** (Moomins, Baymax): adults bond with a companion that is quiet, not hyper. Idle should be 85–90 % still.

**The cute ↔ cool dial [E]:** each row is a separate setting.

| Lever | cute / young ← | → cool / adult (SOUL default) |
|---|---|---|
| where the mass sits | broad end down: drop, cloud, "sitting" (eye line 36–42 %) | broad end up: helmet/head egg (eye line 47–52 %) |
| forehead:chin around the glass | forehead > chin (baby) | chin ≥ 1.6 × forehead (visor) |
| lids | round, fully open | level, 15–25 % lowered; 8–12° V for mischief |
| colour chroma (body) | saturated primaries (C* > 50) | pearl/onyx (C* ≤ 5); drops muted (C* ≤ 25) |
| weight / density | < 50 g, hollow | 95–115 g, ≥ 1.8 g/cm³ |
| motion | bouncy, constant | still, slow-in/out, occasional sharp glance |

## 4. The 10 form rules for SOUL (numbers are targets; [M] = copied from the loved pebble)
**R1 · Orientation comes from the silhouette, not a loop.** Plan H/W = **1.10–1.20** (loved 1.13 [M]). Broad end up: the width at the upper quarter-height is **4–8 % greater** than at the lower quarter (loved +5.7 % [M]). Widest point at **52–56 %** of the height. No pure circle (H/W 1.00): without a bail it has no up.

**R2 · Size and weight are adult, not toy.** Plan **60–64 W × 68–74 H mm**; depth **22–26 mm at the base, 16–18 mm at the top**; **95–115 g** (ρ ≈ 1.8–2.0 with ballast). That is +50–75 % front area vs the Ø52 v1 and still inside the palm-stone envelope (≤ 75 mm, V-06). The plush charms (Fuzozo ~10 cm, BubblePal ping-pong) are light and soft; SOUL wins by being heavy and hard.

**R3 · The face is 52–62 % of the front.** Glass Ø49 on a 62×70 egg ≈ 55 %; 08's Ø52 "infinity glass" ≈ 62 %. This **replaces 08's "≥ 80 %" rule for the upright creature**: that was a coin rule. At 80 %+ the body vanishes, and so does the creature (you get the rejected lens/orb).

**R4 · Helmet placement.** Glass centre at **54–57 %** of the height. Margin top:bottom = **1 : 1.6–2.0** (loved 7.8 : 14.9 mm [M]); side margin at the widest point **5.5–7.5 mm**. The "chin" holds all the mass (cell, ballast, speaker, contacts), so the look and the stance come from the same decision.

**R5 · Eye line and eye geometry.** The eye line sits at **47–52 %** of the total height from the table (loved 50.6 % [M]). Never above 55 % (reads alert/robot/security); never below 38 % (reads toddler). Inside the glass: eyes **6–8 % of Ø below centre**; each eye **29–32 % of the active Ø tall** (≈ 13–14 mm, 135–150 px), W/H **0.72–0.76**, gap **≈ 0.65 × eye width** (≈ 70 px), and the pair spans **≤ 62 %** of Ø, so the eyes never touch the edge and the black stays infinite (08). **Always a catchlight or inner gradient** (standing in for pupils: pupil-less + mouthless + lidded = "surveillance" [V]). **No permanent mouth** (Kitty rule: the owner projects); speech is shown by eye bounce or a transient shape.

**R6 · Attitude = the lid (the șmecher dial).** Default lid: **level, 15–25 % lowered**. Mischief: **8–12° V** (current 8–10° [M]). Hard limit **< 15°** (beyond that it reads angry). Delight or pick-up: eyes open **+10–15 %** for ≤ 1 s. The +15 % cuteness effect [V] is used as a *moment*, not as the resting face. Allow **5–12 % L/R asymmetry** (current 12 % [M]): asymmetry is personality. Eyes cream **#FFF0C8** on true black only; the one accent is the Claude orange (approvals).

**R7 · Softness with one edge.** The silhouette is curvature-continuous (G2), with the **smallest plan radius ≥ 15 mm** (≈ ¼ of the width): no corners anywhere (Bar & Neta). One crisp polished highlight line around the glass; the glass is flush **±0.1 mm**; domed back. **Front = body + glass, nothing else.** Speaker slot on the side or back seam; charging contacts on the base.

**R8 · It sits; it doesn't stand to attention.** Flat base land **24–28 mm wide × 14–18 mm deep** (it carries the pogo contacts). The face leans back **6–10°** (the section tapers from 26 mm at the base to 16 mm at the top; about 8° at 70 mm height), so the eyes meet a seated user's gaze. A seated user's eyes are 40–50 cm above the desk at 50–70 cm → ~30–40° up, and the lean plus an upward-looking eye pose closes that gap [E]. Centre of mass **≤ 40 % of the height**: with 10–20 g of ballast in the chin, the COM lands at ≈ 26 mm on a 70 mm body [E], giving a **static tip angle ≥ 15°**. **Honest limit:** a 1 N finger tap at eye height (≈ 0.035 N·m) beats the ≤ 0.01 N·m restoring moment of any 100 g, 70 mm object on a 16 mm land. So touch happens in the hand or in the capsule; standing free is for voice and glance. **Innovation to prototype: a Daruma base.** A bottom arc with its centre of curvature above the COM makes SOUL *wobble back up* when tapped. That is aliveness with zero motors, and the IMU can make the eyes go dizzy.

**R9 · The body can't move, so the eyes, light and haptics do.** Blinks **6–12/min**, randomised over **3–10 s** intervals, each **120–180 ms** (slightly slower than a human's 100–150 = softer); ~15 % double blinks; sleepy mode **2–4/min** at 300–400 ms (infant-like). Idle **85–90 % still**; eye "breathing" **±1.5 % at 10–14 cycles/min**. Gaze on the user **70–75 % while listening, ~40 % while talking**; glances ~3 s, mutual gaze ~1 s; look away when "thinking". **React within 100 ms** to lift, touch or voice (under the 140 ms causality window). **The soul stays level:** the eyes counter-rotate to stay horizontal within ±30° of roll as you turn the body (IMU; zero BOM cost). Squash and stretch preserve eye area within ±12 %. On a lift, the eyes widen and look up at you.

**R10 · Colour, material, home.** Two core colourways: **Pearl** (gloss, L* ≥ 90, C* ≤ 5; the founder's favourite) and **Onyx** (L* ≤ 15). Seasonal drops are muted (C* ≤ 25) or the Mărțișor red-white edition, never toy primaries. No fur, ears, arms, decals or props. **The capsule is its nest:** SOUL goes home to sleep, the eyes close as it seats, and the contacts on the base touch (LOVOT's Nest, Moflin's bed, the Poké Ball). The capsule, not the body, carries the brand mark.

## 6. Anti-patterns: what turns it into a toy or a creep [E from §1–2]
- **Toy:** fur, ears, arms or wheels as props; primary colours; eyes that turn into hearts, tears or stars; giggles; confetti; begging or sulking; < 50 g hollow plastic; face < 30 % of the front (Eilik: "gadget with a face").
- **Creep:** pupil-less + mouthless + heavy lids + a staring gaze (security-robot read); photoreal eyes (uncanny); motion that is too smooth or too constant; a V lid > 15°; a visible camera (Ropet hides its camera in the nose [V-s]; SOUL has none).
- **Cheap:** screen off = grey rectangle (use AMOLED true black, V-08); visible LCD glow (Loona); a pure circle; matte monotone (v2).

## 7. Open questions / tests (cheap, before CAD freeze)
1. Print three 62×70 eggs at H/W 1.10 / 1.15 / 1.20 with the glass at 54 / 56 % and ballast 0 / 10 / 20 g. Blind-rank "want it" and "looks alive" with 10 people aged 18–35.
2. Lid A/B test on the real panel: level-lowered vs 9° V vs 14° V. Ask "cool / cute / angry?". Target ≥ 60 % "cool", ≤ 10 % "angry".
3. Daruma base: SLA a rounded base with the COM ~5 mm below the centre of curvature and measure the recovery angle. Wobble ≥ 3 cycles = magic; fall-over = kill it.
4. Level-eyes counter-rotation plus the ≤ 100 ms lift reaction: firmware only, film it for the launch.
5. Verify the [K] items (Kodama/Totoro proportions, Cozmo face, Pokémon bedtime voice) before quoting them publicly.
