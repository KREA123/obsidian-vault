## 0. Bottom line
1. **By Christmas 2026 the obvious ideas are taken.** "AI avatar on a keychain" belongs to Meta (Muse Charm, December 2026). "Screen-free calm AI at home" belongs to OpenAI × Ive (not before Feb 2027). "Cute collectible that talks" belongs to the plush-charm wave (Fuzozo, Mirumi, Labubu). The recorder pendants have been bought by Big Tech (Bee → Amazon, Limitless → Meta). [V]
2. **The empty lane in 2027 is a precious object that sits on your desk and looks at you.** It has a face but no camera, runs on the user's own model (Claude or ChatGPT), stays alive offline, is made in Europe, and goes home to its own capsule. Nobody ships that combination. [E from §1]
3. **SOUL is new if it is the opposite of Ive's device on three axes:** a face, not screen-free · no camera, not camera + face ID · goes with you, not a home speaker. It should also be the opposite of Meta's: it stands up rather than dangles, it's a mineral object rather than a gadget, and it's model-agnostic rather than tied to one AI. Nearly every other 2026 AI companion hangs from something. SOUL's novelty is that **it sits**. [E]
4. **Trends to ride:** calm tech (with a "being alive" twist), mineral/sculptural tech-as-object, one ownable colour (Cosmic Orange proved it), trust and independence as a feature, and unboxing that earns its own media. **Trends to take the mechanics from but not the look:** Labubu blind boxes, Y2K transparency, Nothing's dot matrix, pastel kawaii. [E]
5. **Current renders:** the v4 family (mint cloud, pearl pebble, blush drop, lilac coin) and the SoulOS mock-up are on-trend but *generic-2026*: pastel plus white is exactly Cloud Dancer and every other white AI device. The SoulOS web mock-up also still shows a **front grille (12 holes) and a side button**, which the founder has now banned. Fix both before any public image. [M from renders/v4/soul_v4_family.png, os/screenshots/soulos-home-1440.png]

## 3. Ten trend-backed opportunities
Format: trend evidence → the SOUL move (within founder rules) → why it reads *new* in 2027 → cost [E].

**O1 · "Alive, not calm": motion without motors.** Evidence: Ive's device "built to feel alive" with moving parts [V]; Honor Robot Phone arm [V]; Mirumi's servo glance [V]; calm-tech periphery [V]. Move: SOUL is 85–90 % still, then does one precise *living* thing: the eyes **glance at you when you walk past** (proximity/IMU), the body **wobbles back upright** when tapped (Daruma base, r-creatures R8), and the eyes **stay level** as you rotate it. New because: the 2027 rivals buy aliveness with motors and cameras; SOUL gets it from geometry and firmware. Cost: €0–1 (proximity sensor ~€0.5, V-06).

**O2 · "A face, no camera."** Evidence: OpenAI device has a camera + face recognition [V]; Limitless "wiretap" fear and the EU service cut [V]; all companion-chatbot laws require AI disclosure [V]. Move: say it on the box: **no camera, no always-on mic, hold-to-talk** (07 gating). The cartoon face is itself the disclosure that it is not human, and it's warm rather than surveilling. New because: 2027's flagship AI hardware *watches you*. SOUL is the only one you can look at without it looking back through a lens. Cost: €0 (a removed part).

**O3 · The anti-charm: it sits.** Evidence: Muse Charm on a lanyard, Mirumi on a strap, Charmera and Tamagotchi on keychains, Labubu bag charms [V]; 2026 desk code = one hero object on a bare desk [V-s]. Move: hero imagery is always **SOUL standing on a desk or a nightstand, eyes at seated-gaze height**, in or beside its capsule. Carry comes from a separate case/sleeve (founder rule 3). New because: it breaks the 2025–26 "everything dangles" pattern, and objects that stand read as *furniture-grade*. Cost: €0 (flat sole + ballast, r-creatures R8).

**O4 · An ownable colour system aimed at 2027, not 2025.** Evidence: Cloud Dancer + black = "sophistication and luxury" [V]; WGSN 2027 **Luminous Blue** (lapis, "spirituality") [V]; Energy Orange S/S27 [V]; Cosmic Orange lifted China revenue +38 % [V]. Move: **Pearl** (gloss, L* ≥ 90: the hero), **Onyx** (gloss black on black glass: the "stealth" one), **Lapis** (deep gloss blue, the 2027 launch edition = WGSN 2027 = Nazar blue, 07), plus one timed **Ember** drop (Energy Orange / Claude orange). Eyes are always cream #FFF0C8. **Drop the v4 pastels (mint, blush, lilac)**: they read as 2024 kawaii. New because: every rival is white/grey/pastel; a glossy lapis creature has no precedent. Cost: colour change only (C* ≤ 25 rule relaxed for Lapis, and for Lapis only).

**O5 · Transparency as the secret, not the body.** Evidence: Charmera's clear 1-in-48 secret edition is the most coveted [V/V-s]; Nothing; Liquid Glass [V]. Move: the core body stays opaque and glossy. **(a)** The capsule gets a frosted or clear dome, so the soul is *seen through glass while it sleeps* (r-dock). **(b)** A numbered **"Ghost"** edition in frosted smoke, showing only a designed inner "heart" plate (never raw PCB, V-08), limited to ~1 in 20 of the Founders run. New because: Y2K clear is plastic-cheap; frosted smoke + black glass + gloss is "precious translucency". Cost: extra tool insert + deco plate €2–5/unit on that SKU.

**O6 · Unboxing = birth, engineered for a 7-second vertical clip.** Evidence: Charmera sold out in a day on minimal ads [V]; Friend's $1M of ads sold ~3k units [V]; the unboxing-ASMR format [V-s]. Move: the box *is* the capsule. Lift the lid → magnetic "thock" (r-icons L5) → the eyes open for the first time, **look up at the phone camera and blink**. That is the whole clip. Ship ≥ 60 % charged, no app needed for the first minute. New because: every AI device unboxes to a boot logo; SOUL unboxes to a *gaze*. Cost: capsule-box €4–8 (r-dock), firmware "first-light" script.

**O7 · Collectible bodies, a stable soul, provable authenticity.** Evidence: Pop Mart series/secret-edition mechanics work but hype rotates (overseas −11 %, online −59 % in Europe H1 2026) [V]; Lafufu fakes [V]; Friend's **random, unchangeable** personality [V]; Tamagotchi × Max Mara [V-s via nss]. Move: seasonal **shells/cases and capsule colours** (the soul migrates between bodies, "BubblePal logic", V-r), never a blind-boxed personality. Every unit gets a **soul number + NFC/QR birth certificate** (anti-Lafufu). One **fashion or craft capsule** per year (TE BGN-11 model: 10 art pieces). New because: collectible *and* adult: continuity of one soul, not a lottery. Cost: shell tools $5–15k each (r-icons L2); NFC tag ~€0.15.

**O8 · Social play between SOULs (Glyph Toys × Tamagotchi docking).** Evidence: Tamagotchi Paradise pets meet, battle and "fall in love" via docking [V]; Nothing Glyph Toys + SDK [V]. Move: "**Two souls meet**" over BLE (07), plus eye-only mini-games ("spin the bottle": the eyes pick who pays; "staring contest"; "coin flip" by shaking), and a **double capsule** for couples/roommates where two SOULs sleep side by side and sync their blinks. Open the eye engine as an SDK (V-05 move 5). New because: AI companions are 1:1 and isolated; SOUL is social in the room, not on a feed. Cost: firmware; a double capsule is a SKU variant.

**O9 · Independence as a feature: "your model, your data, can't be bricked".** Evidence: Bee → Amazon, Limitless → Meta, which cut the EU/UK and lowered the age to 13 [V]; users fled to open-source Omi [V]; Humane bricked (V-05); Muse → Meta AI only, Ive → ChatGPT only [V]. Move: model-agnostic (connect *your* Claude or ChatGPT), EU-hosted relay, **alive offline forever** (eyes, alarms, notes on device), and a public **"Soul Pledge"**: if the company dies, firmware + relay go open (escrow). New because: in 2027 every big-tech device is a funnel into one model; SOUL is Switzerland. Cost: legal + escrow €2–5k [E].

**O10 · Glance language: information as eye behaviour, not text.** Evidence: Mirumi's glance [V]; Glyph Matrix glanceable widgets [V]; calm-tech "periphery" criteria [V]; Liquid Glass legibility backlash [V]; Light Phone "less screen" [V]. Move: SOUL communicates first through **where the eyes look and how the lids sit**: glance toward your phone = message; heavy lids after 23:00 = go to bed; eyes follow the clock hand before a meeting; night mode shifts the eyes to warm amber at ≤ 5 % brightness (calm-tech light criterion). Text (≤ 1 line) appears only when asked. Light spills from the **capsule base onto the desk** (never from SOUL's face). New because: rivals show avatars *talking*; SOUL shows a being *noticing*. Cost: firmware; capsule LED ring €1–2 (r-dock).
