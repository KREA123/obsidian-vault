# Icons & desire drivers → 12 laws of "want it on sight" for SOUL (2026-09-24)

Tags: **[V]** page fetched today (URL in §6) · **[V-s]** web-search result summary today, page not opened (treat as secondary) · **[V-0x]** verified earlier today in research note 0x · **[K]** background knowledge, verify before quoting publicly · **[E]** estimate / judgement.
Builds on 05 (launch playbook), 06 (reference objects, ergonomics), 07 (amulet grammar, press-the-eye, halo), 08 (CMF, density, infinity glass). Facts already there are cited, not re-derived.
**Founder's hard rules applied to every law below:** front = body + screen only (no grille, button, logo, light, ring) · speaker on sides/back · charging contacts on the bottom + a SOUL dock / "charging capsule" · no loop/bail on the body (carry = separate case) · bigger than the Ø52 v1, hand-filling, portable, **stands upright on a table** · premium, not plastic · a character shape (cloud / coin / **upright glossy pebble ~60×68, screen in the upper part** / drop), not a plain circle, not a flat lying pebble, not a necklace.

## 2. Desire mechanics behind the rows
- **MAYA**, Loewy's "Most Advanced Yet Acceptable" [V]: every hit is *familiar + one new thing*: iPhone = three known devices in one [V]; Switch = console you take with you [V]; Tamagotchi = pet + watch (the name says it) [V]; Watch = crown from watches [V].
- **Kindchenschema** (Lorenz): big head, big eyes set **low** on a round face trigger care; cuteness ratings predict motivation to care; Mickey was redesigned with bigger eyes over time [V]. Tamagotchi's death mechanic proves attachment converts to daily behaviour [V].
- **"Permission to touch"** (Ive on the iMac handle) [V-s]: an organ that invites the hand before the brain decides. Crank, wheel, lid, handle are all invitations.
- **Visible-in-public signal**: white earbuds [V], AirPods [V], Walkman headphones [V]. You want what you saw on someone else at 3 m.
- **Ritual > feature**: pairing on lid-open, sizing kit, try-on appointment, weekly season drop. Brands that design the *first minute* and the *return home* (case, dock) win retention [E].
- **Honest reveal**: Dyson bin, iMac translucency, SK 4 lid, Nothing: show the working *soul*, but only parts that are designed (raw PCB = cheap; 08 ORB warning).
- **Understatement = rank**: Leica removing its own red dot [V-s]; iPhone with nothing but glass on the face [V]. The most premium mark is the absence of marks.

## 3. The 12 laws of "want it on sight" for SOUL
Each: law → evidence → the SOUL move under the hard rules → cost for us [E].

**L1. The face is the whole promise, and nothing else is on it.**
Evidence: iPhone's all-glass face [V]; Leica's stealth Ms delete the logo: "perfect understatement" [V-s]; Playdate "no persistent status bar" [V]; 08: face/plan ≥ 75–80 % reads as jewel, 59 % reads as housing.
SOUL: front = one continuous body surface + one black glass. No seam crosses the front: the parting line runs around the *side/back* edge. Use 08's infinity glass (Ø52–54 black-masked cover over the Ø43.8 panel), so on a ~60×68 upright pebble the dark "eye window" covers ~72 % of the width. The iPhone kept one button; SOUL keeps **zero**: the glass *is* the button (07 press-the-eye / hold-to-talk). Cost: +$2–5 custom glass [V-08 E].

**L2. The silhouette is a character before it powers on.**
Evidence: Tamagotchi egg = "something lives in here", 98M [V]; iMac teardrop drew ~50 % first-time buyers [V]; iPod silhouette ads sold with a black shape + white detail [V]; founder rejected plain circles.
SOUL: pass the **shadow test**: a black silhouette at 20 px must read as SOUL and *not* as a watch or puck. Upright pebble / drop / cloud all pass; a disc fails. Asymmetry top-vs-bottom (narrower head, fuller base) gives up/down with no bail and lets it stand. Keep the family (cloud, coin, pebble, drop) as **collectible shells on one internal chassis**. Cost: ~$0, tooling per shell $5–15k [E].

**L3. Own one colour and one detail recognisable at 3 m.**
Evidence: Bondi blue + "Sorry, no beige" [V/V-s]; iPod white earbuds [V]; Playdate "we could own this yellow" [V]; Leica red dot [V]; TE/r1 orange [V]; AirPods stems [K].
SOUL: the signature detail is **two cream (#FFF0C8) eyes floating in black glass on a pearl body**: that is our "white earbuds". Brand rule: cream appears only as the eyes (and in the capsule's light), never as a sticker. Pearl-glossy white is hero; one dark edition (Onyx). Cost: $0.

**L4. One signature gesture you can name in two words, native to the shape.**
Evidence: iPod wheel (round thing → round gesture) [V]; Playdate crank "zero latency, muscle memory" [V]; Watch crown [V]; AirPods flip [V-s]; iMac handle "permission to touch" [V-s].
SOUL: **"Lift — it looks."** (IMU pick-up → eyes open and search for you) and **"Hold — it listens."** (press-hold anywhere on the glass; mic gated by the press, 07). Both use only body + screen, so the front stays clean. The upright pebble's *hand-shaped back* is the invitation to grab, like the iMac handle. Cost: firmware + one dome/strain sensor ~$1–2 [V-07 E].

**L5. Design the sound: the click is the logo.**
Evidence: Switch Joy-Con snap became the sound logo on every trailer [V-s]; AirPods lid snap = fidget object [V-s]; Apple tuned box-opening emotion with hundreds of prototypes [V-s]; Leica shutter [K].
SOUL: the **capsule "thock"**: magnetic self-centring into the dock with a damped, low click (magnet + felt/silicone stop), followed by a 3-note soft chime from the side/back speaker while the eyes close. Use that exact sound in every film and at first boot. Cost: 2 magnets + damper ~$0.5–1.

**L6. The dock is a home and a stage: design the ritual of return.**
Evidence: Switch dock turns handheld into TV [V]; AirPods case = carry + charge + ritual [V/K]; B&O doors open as your hand approaches [V-s]; Watch nightstand mode on its charger [K].
SOUL: the **charging capsule** is half the product. Bottom contacts (pogo targets in a flat foot, founder rule 2) sit in a weighted cradle that holds SOUL upright facing you. Docked = bedside clock / sleepy eyes; **hand approaches → eyes open** (B&O trick with the existing touch/IMU or a $0.5 proximity sensor). Optional frosted cloche lid or AirPods-style carry capsule (with the strap/loop the body isn't allowed to have, rule 3). Cost: cradle $4–8 (06); carry capsule with its own cell +$6–10 [E].

**L7. Show the soul, never the circuit.**
Evidence: Dyson bin shown against research advice, then copied everywhere [V]; iMac translucency [V]; SK 4 plexiglass lid [V]; Nothing = designed internals [V/K]; counter: Friend yellowing white plastic (V-07), 08's ORB warning about raw ESP32 internals.
SOUL: what we reveal is **light and life**, not the PCB: eyes that move when you walk past; a faint glow in the capsule; a translucent/frosted *capsule*, not a transparent body. Cost: $0–2.

**L8. It must feel more expensive than it looks: density, coolness, one precise edge.**
Evidence: iPhone ≈ 1.66 g/cm³ (V-08 calc); iPod steel back [V]; Leica metal and bayonet [V]; counter: Vision Pro 600–650 g + 353 g → "heavy", record returns, production halted [V/V-s].
SOUL: 85–110 g in ~45–55 cm³ ⇒ **~2 g/cm³** (08); glossy pearl zirconia / ceramic-look back with one diamond-cut highlight edge around the glass; zero flex, dull "tock" when tapped. Heavy enough to stand firmly, light enough to pocket. Cost: ballast plate $1–2; ceramic edition per 08.

**L9. Cute face, adult body.**
Evidence: Kindchenschema: big, low eyes on a round face trigger care [V]; Tamagotchi attachment [V]; counters: EyeSight "uncanny" [V]; Friend "Life Alert" look (V-07); 08: toy accessories read as kids' gadgets.
SOUL: eyes big, round-ish, placed **slightly below the glass centre** (baby-schema), no mouth, no brows; blinking and gaze do the emotion. The body stays mineral: glossy pearl, no ears, no decals. Cost: firmware.

**L10. MAYA + withered technology: familiar form, cheap mature parts, one new behaviour.**
Evidence: Loewy MAYA [V]; Yokoi's "withered technology" beat colour rivals on battery and price [V]; Walkman was a known cassette made personal [V]; counter: OP-1's single AMOLED source stopped production and forced +35 % [V].
SOUL: familiar = a pebble / egg / pet / bedside clock; new = **it looks back at you and remembers**. Mature tech: ESP32-S3 + 1.75" AMOLED (V-06). Innovation budget goes into form, eyes and ritual, not silicon. **Dual-source the round AMOLED** before DVT. Cost: BOM stays ~€50–70 @1k (V-06).

**L11. The first minute is the product: unboxing = birth.**
Evidence: Apple's packaging room [V-s]; AirPods pair on lid-open [V]; Game Boy playable instantly with Tetris [V]; Playdate book-box "cool enough to keep" [V]; Oura sizing kit / Watch try-on = ritual before purchase [V-s/V]; counters: Vision Pro launched without core apps [V]; r1 exposed as "an app" [V].
SOUL: book-sized box → the capsule inside → lift SOUL out → **its eyes open for the first time and find you**, offline, no app, no account. Ship charged (≥60 %). One pull-tab, no plastic bags, birth certificate card (07). Cost: box $3–6 [E].

**L12. Wow sells the first one; a daily reason sells the next thousand.**
Evidence: r1 ~5 % daily use [V]; Humane returns > sales [V]; Watch saved by health [V]; Oura sleep score + 1-week battery [V]; Playdate seasons "there's a new game today!" [V]; Oura/Humane subscription backlash [V].
SOUL: three daily touchpoints: **morning** lift → today card; **day** hold-to-talk notes, reminders, Claude approvals; **night** capsule → sleepy eyes, alarm. ≥2-day battery (V-06/07). Core life + offline eyes free forever; paid tier only for heavy cloud AI. Never replace the phone (Humane). Cost: firmware/cloud.

## 5. What this means for the SOUL body (one screen) [E]
- **Front:** pearl body + infinity-black glass, nothing else. Eyes set low in the glass.
- **Sides:** the parting line doubles as a **speaker slot** (hairline, tuned) on one side; the other side stays clean.
- **Back:** hand-shaped convex back (the "handle"); engraved soul number, the only text on the object (07 private back).
- **Bottom:** flat foot so it stands upright + 2 pogo targets + alignment magnets.
- **Capsule:** weighted cradle, magnetic "thock", proximity-wake, bedside mode; carry capsule with a strap as the *only* hanging solution.
- **Signature line (for the film):** "Lift it — it looks at you. Hold it — it listens. Put it to bed — it dreams."
