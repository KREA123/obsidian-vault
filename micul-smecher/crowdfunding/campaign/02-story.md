### it looks back.

No app to open, no account, no internet needed for the eyes. Everything you see on the glass runs on the stone, and keeps running if the Wi-Fi never comes back.

- **it blinks.** At random, never on a timer. Sometimes twice, when it's curious.
- **it follows your finger.** Slide a finger on the glass and the eyes follow it. Leave it alone and it dozes off.
- **it has moods.** 31 expressions: happy, sleepy, suspicious, surprised, dizzy when you shake it, and a few we'll let you find.

### born with one of 120 eyes.

At first power-on, each SOUL is born from its chip's unique ID. The eyes it gets are the eyes it keeps. Nobody picks them. Not even us.

{{img:eyes-grid}}
*The SOUL eyes engine, drawn from the same code that runs on the device. Screen images, not photos.*

Some pairs are much rarer than others. These are the real odds from the birth code, the same math that runs on every SOUL:

| rarity | chance at birth | designs | chance of one exact pair |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 50 % | 48 | 1 in 96 |
| Uncommon | 25 % | 30 | 1 in 120 |
| Rare | 15 % | 20 | 1 in 133 |
| Epic | 7 % | 12 | 1 in 171 |
| Legendary | 2.5 % | 7 | 1 in 280 |
| Mythic | 0.5 % | 3 | 1 in 600 |

Inside a rarity, every design is equally likely. **Rarity changes only the eyes**: every SOUL does exactly the same things. You can't choose, buy, swap or re-roll eyes, and no reward on this page costs more for a rarer pair. You choose the colour of the body; the eyes choose themselves.

Want to see it for yourself? Type any chip ID into our lookup and see exactly what that chip would be born as: {{URL:chip-lookup}}

Each SOUL's screens also take their colours from the eyes it was born with and from its aluminium body. The chance that two SOULs share the same pair of eyes is about 1 in 112.

Hold your finger on the glass and SOUL listens. Let go and it answers on the glass. Or type on its round keyboard. The two microphones are on **only while your finger is on the glass**. No wake word. Lift your finger and it stops.

### SoulOS Alive: the eyes are the os.

Touch the glass and the eyes glide up to become the face of every screen. Pure black, flat colour, chunky round shapes: the same language as the eyes and the stone. On SOUL S it runs on a round 1.75″ AMOLED, where black is truly black, so the eyes float on the glass.

{{img:soulos-dial}}
*SoulOS web prototype capture, 480 × 480; on S it runs on a 466 × 466 AMOLED.*

- **a dial made for a circle.** Your apps sit around the eyes like small pebbles. Turn the ring with a finger. Nothing gets cut off by the round edge.
- **the eyes stay with you.** Open a note, a timer or your AI: the eyes move to the top of the circle and keep reacting.
- **apps open with a blink.** Two black lids close for a moment, the screen changes behind them, and they open on your app.
- **standby that doesn't stare.** No clock, no glow, no notifications. In standby, SOUL shows only its eyes on pure black.
