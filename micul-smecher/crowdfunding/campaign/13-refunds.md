You can cancel or change your pledge at any time from your Indiegogo account. Nothing is charged until the campaign ends successfully.

**After the campaign, any time before your SOUL ships**
You can cancel and get a **full refund** of everything you paid (reward and shipping), without giving a reason. Write to office@krea.ro or use Indiegogo's order page. We refund within 14 days of your request. We keep a refund reserve in the budget for exactly this.

**If we miss the estimated delivery month**
Each reward shows an estimated delivery month. If your SOUL hasn't shipped by the end of that month, we tell you in an update and give a new date at most 30 days later. If we miss that one too, you can end your pledge and get a full refund, even if you don't write to us first about the new date. You can also cancel at any time before shipping anyway (above).

**If something important changes**
If a specification that matters changes (size, screen, battery, features), we announce it in an update before shipping. You can cancel for a full refund at any time before your SOUL ships.
