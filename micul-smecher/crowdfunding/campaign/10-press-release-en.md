## SOUL, a palm-sized aluminium stone with living eyes that works with your own AI, launches on Indiegogo

*Made by Bucharest's ARTEMIS DIGITAL, SOUL connects to the owner's own Claude or OpenAI API key, or runs offline. It has no camera and listens only while a finger is on the glass.*

**Bucharest, <!--f:launch_day-->Tuesday 5 October 2027<!--/f-->.** ARTEMIS DIGITAL S.R.L. today opens the Indiegogo campaign for **SOUL**, a palm-sized aluminium object with two living eyes on a round black glass. SOUL blinks, follows a finger on the glass, shows 31 expressions and dozes off when left alone. It talks with the AI its owner already uses (their own Claude, through an Anthropic API key or the Claude app connector, or their own OpenAI API key, in development), or lives entirely offline.

SOUL does not include AI, and ARTEMIS does not sell it. Owners connect their own Anthropic API key and can add SOUL as a custom connector in the Claude app, so Claude can put a plan, a reminder or a note on SOUL for the owner to approve on the glass. Support for an owner's own OpenAI key is in development. Offline, the eyes, moods, notes, reminders, alarms and focus run on the device, with no account.

**Born with its eyes.** At first power-on, each SOUL is "born" from its chip's unique ID with one of 120 eye designs across six rarities, from Common (50 %) to Mythic (0.5 %). Nobody can choose, buy or re-roll a pair, and the company publishes the exact odds from the birth code. Rarity changes only the eyes; every SOUL does the same things.

**Privacy as promises, not settings.** SOUL has no camera. Its two microphones are on only while the owner's finger is on the glass; there is no wake word. SOUL Memory, what SOUL remembers about its owner, lives on the device in plain words that can be read and deleted. If the company ever disappears, it commits to publishing the firmware so the device keeps working.

**The object.** SOUL S measures 63 × 71 mm, with a depth of <!--f:depth-->23–26 mm<!--/f--> still being finalised. Its shape, MĂRGĂRITAR, is machined from aluminium 6061, bead-blasted and anodised in <!--f:colours-->Silver, Graphite and Ember<!--/f-->, with a round 1.75-inch AMOLED (466 × 466) under black glass. It charges in OU, a charging egg, through contacts in its foot. Its interface, SoulOS Alive, is built around the eyes: apps sit on a round dial and open with a blink.
