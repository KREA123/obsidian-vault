# Rewards (Indiegogo "Products → Rewards / Add-ons")

The active ladder comes from `facts.json → campaign.ladder` and `pricing.ladders` (decision D3). The machine-readable version, column by column for Indiegogo's form, is `perks.csv` (generated by `tools/build_copy.py`). Don't edit the f-blocks by hand.

## How to enter them

- **Price field:** put the campaign price in **Regular price** and leave **Discount price** empty. Never show the planned retail price as a struck-through "old" price, a "% off" badge or a "save €x" line: SOUL has never been sold at that price, so it would be a reference price we can't support under EU price-indication rules (Omnibus Directive). The planned retail price is said in words in each description ("Planned retail price after the campaign: …"), and nowhere else.
- **Tax rates: set every EU tax rate to 0 %, in all cases.** Indiegogo treats reward prices as **net** and pre-fills EU VAT rates (HC/478, HC/597). If they stay, backers are charged VAT on top of the published price. ARTEMIS isn't VAT-registered today, so it may not charge VAT at all; if it registers (D2), VAT is inside the published price and ARTEMIS remits it through OSS. Either way the backer pays exactly the published price. Check it in **Tester mode**: the checkout total must equal the reward price (plus shipping in the Pledge Manager), with no tax line.
- **Colour** is an **option** on the SOUL item (HC/526), <!--f:colours_n-->three<!--/f--> values (<!--f:colours-->Silver, Graphite and Ember<!--/f-->), no price difference, no per-colour caps at launch. SKUs from `perks.csv`.
- **Eyes** are never an option, a reward, an add-on or a stretch goal.
- **Batches (D12).** Batch 1 is <!--f:batch1-->270<!--/f--> SOULs, the batch the delivery timeline is planned for. First Light, Campaign Price and Two Souls are **capped so together they fill batch 1 exactly**. "Campaign Price · batch 2" has the same price and a later delivery month, so nobody who pledges under one date is moved to a later one.
- **Estimated delivery:** one month and year per reward: <!--f:delivery_field-->May 2028 (EU: May 2028 (estimate)); Campaign Price · batch 2: August 2028 (Batch 2, EU: August 2028 (estimate))<!--/f-->.
- **Availability by location** (HC/528): allow only RO, DE, AT, FR, BE, NL, IT, ES, PL, SE on device rewards (UK: decision D13; US/Canada: D14). Soul Supporter: worldwide.
- **Pin** First Light while it lasts, then Two Souls. Only one pin at a time.
- **Stretch Pay:** off during the campaign. **Stable Pledge:** turn on at G3 only if courier and 3PL quotes are in writing (it is irreversible, HC/471).
- The last line of each device description is "Render (CGI), not a photo." because the reward image is a render.

Not in this campaign: the UK, the US, Canada, Switzerland, Norway, Australia and the rest of the world. They need their own certification, registrations or tax set-up first; we'll open them in Late Pledge only when that's done.

SOUL contains a lithium battery, so it ships by road or courier as "lithium battery in equipment" (UN3481), at low charge. Charge it in OU when it arrives.

## Why this ladder (internal, don't publish)

- One capped launch price, one campaign price, then a clearly higher Late Pledge: fewer, wider steps than the first draft.
- **First Light** is more than €20 off: it carries its First Light number (001–150) laser-engraved on the foot. The laser mark is already in the unit cost (serial, CE, WEEE), so it costs nothing extra [estimate]. It is not a rarity and changes nothing else.
- **Two Souls** costs the First Light price per unit (€249 each), so the gift tier has a real pull; its contribution is the same as First Light. Capped at 10 sets in batch 1.
- **No cut to the single-unit price**: contribution at First Light is only about €49 per device. The price is justified as a machined-aluminium design object: macro footage of the bead-blasted finish is on the G2 shot list (video/script.md).
- Rarity is never part of pricing.
