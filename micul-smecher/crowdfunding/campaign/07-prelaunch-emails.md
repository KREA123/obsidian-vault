## Email 2 · Born, not made

**Subject:** Nobody picks its eyes. Not even us.
**Preview:** 120 designs, six rarities, and the real odds.

Hi,

when a SOUL is switched on for the first time, it's **born**. Its chip has a unique ID, and that ID decides which of **120 pairs of eyes** it gets. The eyes it gets are the eyes it keeps.

{{img:eyes-alive}}
*The SOUL eyes engine, recorded from the same code that runs on the device.*

Some pairs are much rarer than others. Here are the real odds from the birth code:

- Common 50 % (48 designs)
- Uncommon 25 % (30)
- Rare 15 % (20)
- Epic 7 % (12)
- Legendary 2.5 % (7)
- Mythic 0.5 % (3)

Rarity changes only the eyes. Every SOUL does exactly the same things, and nobody will ever be able to buy, choose or re-roll a pair. Not in the campaign, not after.

Try it: type any chip ID and see what it would be born as. → {{URL:chip-lookup}}

Then reply with the name of the pair you got. We're curious too.

## Email 3 · Your AI, your rules

**Subject:** Your AI has a brain. Give it a soul.
**Preview:** Bring your own Claude or OpenAI key. Or none.

Hi,

every day, you talk to AI in a chat box. SOUL gives it a body.

**SOUL doesn't include AI, and we'll never sell it to you.** It's being built to connect to the one you already use, on your own account:

- **your Claude:** your own Anthropic API key, and SOUL as a custom connector in the Claude app (being built). The plan: ask Claude to put tomorrow's plan on your SOUL, and it waits on the glass for your tap.
- **your OpenAI key:** your own OpenAI API key (in development); a ChatGPT Plus subscription isn't a key and doesn't work. "Sign in with ChatGPT" is pending OpenAI's approval, so we don't promise it.
- **offline:** no AI, no sign-up. The eyes, the moods, notes, reminders, alarms and focus all live on the stone.

You pay them directly, at their prices. No SOUL subscription.

**SOUL Memory** lives on the device: names, plans, the things you told it. Switch AIs and it still knows you. (Voice is planned too: today it needs your own OpenAI key; voice for Claude-only owners is in development.)

And the promises stay the same: no camera, it listens only while your finger is on the glass, and if we ever disappear, we publish the firmware. It never becomes a brick.

Hold the glass,
