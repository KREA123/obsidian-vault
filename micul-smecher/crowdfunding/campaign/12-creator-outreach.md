# Creator and reviewer outreach

No names here on purpose: the list (`press-list.csv`) is built at **G2**, when there's a real S to show, from creators who meet the criteria below. **No unit is lent before the EU Declaration of Conformity is signed** (CE gate, target 31 August 2027): lending non-CE radio equipment in the EU counts as making it available on the market, even free of charge (Blue Guide). Before the DoC, creators may only see a unit in a demo supervised by ARTEMIS. Nobody is contacted before G2, and no creator, outlet or quote appears anywhere on our page until it has actually been published.

## Who we look for

**Tiers** (PLAN.md §8.3):
1. **Romanian tech and business media, podcasts, startup newsletters.** Angle: an aluminium AI object designed in Bucharest, honest odds and budget.
2. **EU design and gadget media.** Angle: the object (MĂRGĂRITAR, anodised colours, AMOLED eyes).
3. **YouTube / TikTok / Instagram creators:** AI hardware, desk setups, minimal tech, ESP32 / maker builds.

**Criteria for every creator** (check each before adding to the list):
- Publishes regularly in the last 90 days, in English, Romanian or German (we ship to the campaign countries only).
- Has covered at least one of: AI gadgets, desk accessories, design objects, privacy-first tech, ESP32/maker projects.
- Audience mostly in our shipping countries (RO, DE, AT, FR, BE, NL, IT, ES, PL, SE), judged from their content language and stated location.
- Discloses sponsorships (#ad or the platform's paid-partnership label) in past videos. Skip anyone who doesn't.
- Has reviewed hardware critically before (has said "no" or "not worth it" on camera at least once). We want honest reviews.
- Size: no minimum. A 10k-subscriber channel about desk setups that fits beats a 1M general channel.
- Not a crowdfunding "review mill" that only praises campaigns for a fee.

**Log per creator in `press-list.csv`:** channel URL, platform, language, country, topic fit (1–3), last upload date, disclosure practice (yes/no), contact route (public business email or contact form only), status, date contacted, follow-up date, outcome.

Contact only through a **public business email or contact form**. Never DM personal accounts repeatedly, never buy contact lists.

## What we offer

- A **pre-production SOUL S on loan** (DVT unit), with a prepaid return label, **only after the EU DoC is signed**. Limited: 5 units in the first round [[CE: confirm the count and the DoC date]].
- The press kit: real one-take video, labelled renders, true-scale size graphic, specs from `facts.json`, the risks text.
- A 20-minute call with the founder, if they want one.
- **Embargo:** <!--f:launch_day-->Tuesday 5 October 2027<!--/f-->, 09:00 Bucharest time (08:00 Berlin, 07:00 London).

## What we never do
- Pay for a positive review, or make a review conditional on anything.
- Ask to approve or edit their content. We correct only factual errors, if they ask.
- Send a render and call it the product, or an M unit and call it S.
- Offer affiliate commissions without the creator disclosing them. (If we ever run a referral link via Indiegogo, HC/488, it's disclosed as #ad / affiliate.)
- Give anyone a better pair of eyes, a "rare" unit or a unit picked for its eyes.
