born with one of 120 eyes.

At first power-on, each SOUL is born from its chip's unique ID. The eyes it gets are the eyes it keeps. Nobody picks them. Not even us.

[IMAGE: story_02_eyes.png]

The SOUL eyes engine, drawn from the same code that runs on the device. Screen images, not photos.

Some pairs are much rarer than others. These are the real odds from the birth code, the same math that runs on every SOUL:

rarity chance at birth designs chance of one exact pair
Common 50 % 48 1 in 96
Uncommon 25 % 30 1 in 120
Rare 15 % 20 1 in 133
Epic 7 % 12 1 in 171
Legendary 2.5 % 7 1 in 280
Mythic 0.5 % 3 1 in 600

Inside a rarity, every design is equally likely. Rarity changes only the eyes: every SOUL does exactly the same things. You can't choose, buy, swap or re-roll eyes, and no reward on this page costs more for a rarer pair. You choose the colour of the body; the eyes choose themselves.

Want to see it for yourself? Type any chip ID into our lookup and see exactly what that chip would be born as: {{URL:chip-lookup}}

Each SOUL's screens also take their colours from the eyes it was born with and from its aluminium body. The chance that two SOULs share the same pair of eyes is about 1 in 112.

Eyes

Can I choose the eyes? No, and that's the fun. You choose the colour of the body. The eyes are decided once, at first power-on, from the chip's unique ID: one of 120 designs. Nobody picks them. Not even us.

What are the odds? Common 50 % (48 designs), Uncommon 25 % (30), Rare 15 % (20), Epic 7 % (12), Legendary 2.5 % (7), Mythic 0.5 % (3). Inside a rarity, every design is equally likely. These are the real odds from the birth code; the same math runs on every SOUL. You can type any chip ID into our lookup to see what it would be born as: {{URL:chip-lookup}}

Does a rarer pair do more? No. Rarity changes only the eyes. Every SOUL does exactly the same things, and no reward costs more for a rarer pair.

Can I buy, swap or re-roll eyes later? No. The eyes it's born with are the eyes it keeps. We'll never sell eyes, re-rolls or rarity.

Do Two Souls get matching eyes? Each SOUL is born with its own eyes, so they rarely match: the chance that two share the same pair is about 1 in 112.

The object

How big is it? SOUL S is 63 × 71 mm, 23–26 mm deep (the exact depth is being finalised), with a round 1.75″ AMOLED of 466 × 466 pixels under black glass. Weight is ~75–95 g (estimate until we weigh the real S).

Why do some videos show a bigger SOUL? Our first working prototype is size M (90 × 101 mm, 2.8″ screen). The SOUL you back is S. Anything on the page that shows M says so. Renders of the object alone don't show scale; the size drawing is true scale.

Why AMOLED? On AMOLED, black pixels are off, so black is truly black. The eyes float on the glass and standby shows nothing but the eyes.

What is it made of? Aluminium 6061, machined, bead-blasted and anodised, in Silver, Graphite and Ember. Every colour costs the same. We designed five finishes; Midnight and Champagne aren't offered in this campaign. The front is one round black glass, nothing else: no logo, no buttons.

How does it charge? In OU, its charging egg, through gold contacts in its foot. No cable on the stone. OU takes a USB-C cable (in the box) and any USB-C phone charger.

How long does the battery last? We'll publish battery life only after we measure it on the real S unit, and before we ship. [[G3: insert the measured figure and the method]] The battery capacity is being finalised.

Can the battery be replaced? It's designed to be: a screwed back, a connector, no glue, as the new EU Battery Regulation requires. We'll keep spare batteries available.

Does it work away from home? Offline it's always alive. For AI it needs Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz), and it can use your phone's hotspot.

Is it waterproof? No. Keep it dry.

Is it a toy? No. SOUL is for people aged 18 and over. It contains small parts and a lithium battery.

Privacy

Does it have a camera? No. There is no camera in SOUL.

Does it listen all the time? No. It listens only while your finger is on the glass. No wake word. Asleep in OU, it doesn't listen at all.

Where does my data go? SOUL Memory lives on the device, in plain words you can read and delete. When you talk to your AI, your words go to the provider you connected, on your account. Offline, nothing leaves the stone. Some connections (the Claude app connector, or a key you choose to keep in our cloud) pass through SOUL Cloud, our own relay; the privacy notice says what it keeps and for how long: {{URL:privacy}}. No ads, and we never sell your data.

What if ARTEMIS disappears? SOUL's personality runs on the device, and it works offline. If we ever disappear, we publish the firmware so SOUL never becomes a brick.
