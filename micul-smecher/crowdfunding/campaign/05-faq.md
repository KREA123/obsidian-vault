## The basics

**What is SOUL?**
A palm-sized aluminium stone with two living eyes on a round black glass. It blinks, follows your finger, has moods and dozes off when left alone. It works offline, and it can talk with your own Claude or your own OpenAI API key (in development). No camera, and it listens only while your finger is on the glass.

**Does SOUL include AI? Is there a subscription?**
No and no. SOUL connects to your own Claude (API key, or the Claude app connector) or your own OpenAI API key (in development), and you pay Anthropic or OpenAI directly for what you use. Or you use it offline, with no AI at all. We never sell, include or resell AI, and there's no SOUL subscription.

**Which AI does it work with?**
- **Claude:** your own Anthropic API key (from the Anthropic Console), plus SOUL as a custom connector in the Claude app, so Claude can put a plan, a reminder or a note on your SOUL (you approve it on the glass).
- **OpenAI:** your own OpenAI API key. In development.
- **"Sign in with ChatGPT"** is pending OpenAI's approval. We don't control that, so we don't promise it.
- **Offline:** no AI, no sign-up, ever.
Claude is a trademark of Anthropic; ChatGPT and OpenAI are trademarks of OpenAI. SOUL is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by either.

**Do I need a Claude Pro or ChatGPT Plus subscription?**
No, and they don't work with SOUL on their own: those subscriptions aren't API keys, and SOUL never asks for your Claude or ChatGPT password. You need your own API key from Anthropic or OpenAI (pay-as-you-go), or the Claude app for the connector. We'll publish a step-by-step guide, including how to set a monthly spending limit.

**What does SOUL do without any AI?**
It's alive: the eyes, 31 expressions, the moods, notes, reminders, alarms, a timer and focus all run on the stone. Type on its round keyboard. Nothing leaves the device.

**Can I talk to it?**
Hold your finger on the glass and speak; let go and it answers on the glass. The two microphones are on only while your finger is on the glass. <!--f:voice-->One honest detail: turning your voice into text needs a speech service. Today SoulOS uses OpenAI's, with your own OpenAI key. Voice for owners who only use Claude is in development. Typing on its round keyboard always works, with any AI and offline.<!--/f--> [[G1: update with what works on the S board]]

**Does it talk back out loud?**
SOUL S has a speaker. Spoken answers are in development; today answers appear on the glass. We'll say in an update when spoken answers work on the real S. [[G2: update]]

## Eyes

**Can I choose the eyes?**
No, and that's the fun. You choose the colour of the body. The eyes are decided once, at first power-on, from the chip's unique ID: one of 120 designs. Nobody picks them. Not even us.

**What are the odds?**
Common 50 % (48 designs), Uncommon 25 % (30), Rare 15 % (20), Epic 7 % (12), Legendary 2.5 % (7), Mythic 0.5 % (3). Inside a rarity, every design is equally likely. These are the real odds from the birth code; the same math runs on every SOUL. You can type any chip ID into our lookup to see what it would be born as: {{URL:chip-lookup}}

**Does a rarer pair do more?**
No. Rarity changes only the eyes. Every SOUL does exactly the same things, and no reward costs more for a rarer pair.

**Can I buy, swap or re-roll eyes later?**
No. The eyes it's born with are the eyes it keeps. We'll never sell eyes, re-rolls or rarity.

**Do Two Souls get matching eyes?**
Each SOUL is born with its own eyes, so they rarely match: the chance that two share the same pair is about 1 in 112.

## The object

**How big is it?**
SOUL S is 63 × 71 mm, <!--f:depth-->23–26 mm<!--/f--> deep (the exact depth is being finalised), with a round 1.75″ AMOLED of 466 × 466 pixels under black glass. Weight is <!--f:weight-->~75–95 g<!--/f--> (estimate until we weigh the real S).

**Why do some videos show a bigger SOUL?**
Our bench prototype is size M (90 × 101 mm, 2.8″ screen), being built now: the board is ordered, not yet tested. The SOUL you back is S. Anything on the page that shows M says so. Renders of the object alone don't show scale; the size drawing is true scale.

**Why AMOLED?**
On AMOLED, black pixels are off, so black is truly black. The eyes float on the glass and standby shows nothing but the eyes.

**What is it made of?**
Aluminium 6061, machined, bead-blasted and anodised, in <!--f:colours-->Silver, Graphite and Ember<!--/f-->. Every colour costs the same. We designed five finishes; <!--f:colours_later-->Midnight and Champagne<!--/f--> aren't offered in this campaign. The front is one round black glass, nothing else: no logo, no buttons.

**How does it charge?**
In OU, its charging egg, through gold contacts in its foot. No cable on the stone. OU takes a USB-C cable (in the box) and any USB-C phone charger.

**How long does the battery last?**
We'll publish battery life only after we measure it on the real S unit, and before we ship. [[G3: insert the measured figure and the method]] The battery capacity is being finalised.

**Can the battery be replaced?**
It's designed to be: a screwed back, a connector, no glue, as the new EU Battery Regulation requires. We'll keep spare batteries available.

**Does it work away from home?**
Offline it's always alive. For AI it needs Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz), and it can use your phone's hotspot.

**Is it waterproof?**
No. Keep it dry.

**Is it a toy?**
No. SOUL is for people aged <!--f:age-->18<!--/f--> and over. It contains small parts and a lithium battery.

## Privacy

**Does it have a camera?**
No. There is no camera in SOUL.

**Does it listen all the time?**
No. It listens only while your finger is on the glass. No wake word. Asleep in OU, it doesn't listen at all.

**Where does my data go?**
SOUL Memory lives on the device, in plain words you can read and delete. When you talk to your AI, your words go to the provider you connected, on your account. Offline, nothing leaves the stone. Some connections (the Claude app connector, or a key you choose to keep in our cloud) pass through SOUL Cloud, our own relay; the privacy notice says what it keeps and for how long: {{URL:privacy}}. No ads, and we never sell your data.

**What if ARTEMIS disappears?**
SOUL's personality runs on the device, and it works offline. If we ever disappear, we publish the firmware so SOUL never becomes a brick.
