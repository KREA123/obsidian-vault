# SOUL S on the firmware: SoulOS Alive on the 1.75" AMOLED

Decision 2026-10-20 (`03 Decizii/Jurnal decizii.md`): the product is **SOUL S**. It is the MĂRGĂRITAR at its native
scale, with a round **1.75" AMOLED, 466 × 466** on the **Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75** (`pio run -e amoled175`).
The working prototype stays **SOUL M**, the Waveshare 2.8C (`env:lcd28`, firmware 1.9.0 "SoulOS Alive"), for testing
and the PoC TRL 3 proof. This page covers firmware step 1: the Alive core on S.

## 2 · The density layer (`lib/Suflet/src/Density.h`, `Density.cpp`)

SoulOS was laid out in "design px" of a 466 grid, and `DisplayGeometry` scaled that grid by pixel count (× 1.03 to 480).
Scaled by pixel count alone, S would show every screen 36 % smaller in the hand: an 18 px tag 1.7 mm tall, a 44 px
button 4.1 mm wide. `Density::forGlass(geometry)` now gives every board a set of tokens:

- **Physical tokens (kept in mm).** Type floor 18 px on M = 2.63 mm → **28 px** on S. Secondary words 20 px = 2.92 mm
  → **31 px**. Touch target 44 px = 6.43 mm → **68 px**.
- **The type ramp.** Above the floor, sizes are compressed: `s = round(28 · (m / 18)^0.7)`. That gives tag 28 (Martian
  Mono), caption 32, body 37, name 40 and title 45 (Bricolage). The atlases are generated by `tools/gen_font_alive.py s`
  into `FontAliveSData.h` (194 KB of flash, linked only in non-2.8C builds). The time picker's 104 px digits are
  shared. `fonts::setRamp()` is set by `Os::begin`. `ts` = 37/27 and `cs` = 32/22 are the scales that boxes holding
  words grow by.
- **Fitted tokens** are solved from the ones above. For S they come out as:

**SOUL M is the reference glass.** `forGlass(kLcd28)` returns the 1.9.0 constants bit for bit (`test_density.cpp`
compares the structs with `memcmp`). The 2.8C build draws **pixel-identical** frames. `tools/perf_bench.py --hashes`
was run before and after on every scenario, all 120 designs at standby, the app stills, the 58 `screens` stills and
the 30 `alive` stills: **265 hash files, 26 042 frames, IDENTICAL**.

## 3 · AMOLED: true black and burn-in care

On S, black is pixels off. Standby (the eyes alone on `#000`) lights only the eyes, so it costs little. The eye whites
are also the only pixels that could burn in. `Geometry.h` marks the AMOLEDs `emissive`. What follows applies only to
them; the 2.8C's IPS is unchanged.

1. **Drift.** On standby and asleep, the whole face wanders on a Lissajous of ±6 px with periods of 7 and 11 min
   (`burnin::drift`, in `Os::layoutFor`). It moves in whole pixels, one pixel every ≥ 10 s, so there are no extra
   sub-pixel repaints.
2. **Life that already moves.** Saccades and glances every 1–4 s (`Brain::updateIdle`), micro-saccades and blinks
   (`EyeRig`) and the breathing sleep keep the pupils and lids moving.
3. **Night dimming.** The existing levels in `Os::backlight`: 0.35 at night, a 0.06 glow asleep at night, 0.18 by day.
4. **Lights out.** After 20 min asleep at night (60 min by day), `Os::backlightAsleep` returns 0 and the panel's
   brightness goes to 0. A touch, a pick-up or a voice wakes SOUL as before.
5. **Nothing static.** No clock, rings or hints on standby (DESIGN-ALIVE §1). Rim capsules only show while awake.

[estimare] AMOLED burn-in depends on the panel's emitter mix and its hours at high brightness. The drift is the
standard mitigation for always-on faces. A soak test (BRINGUP §6, to add: 72 h standby, then a full grey field
photographed) decides whether the drift amplitude or the lights-out times need tuning.

## 4 · Voice on S: hold the glass = talk

The S board has two mics (ES7210 ADC) and a speaker (ES8311 DAC + PA on GPIO 46). The firmware does not drive these
codecs yet (`src/audio.cpp`: the only mic path is the 2.8C's I2S INMP441). `audioHasMic()` is therefore false on
`amoled175`, and `Os::setVoiceAvailable(false)`. Holding the glass then shows "Let go, then type" and opens the
keyboard. This is the same path as an M without the mic, and it is tested. To do: an ES8311/ES7210 driver over I2C
plus I2S (TDM for the ES7210), from Espressif's `esp_codec_dev` register sequences, checked on the board. It feeds the
existing `audioRecord` / `audioTake` / `netTranscribe` / ESP-SR path, and `SUFLET_VOICE` then turns STT on.

## 5 · What is on S now, and the screens step

**Core screens done, audited at S's floors** (`test_audit_soul_s_core_screens`: 10 screens, 0 failures). They are
standby, wake, the dial, the wheels (Settings, Alarms, World clock), the AI's thinking and answer, Claude asks, and
the notification banner. Stills and the true-size sheet against M are in `sim/shots/alive-s/`
(`.pio/build/sim/program DIR alive-s; python3 tools/alive_s_sheet.py DIR sim/shots/alive-s`).

**The screens step** gets its to-do list from `AUDIT_S_ALL=1 .pio/build/native/program`, which runs every screen on S
and prints the result without asserting it. As of this step:

- **Targets under 68 px:** timer presets (58), Music controls (64), My SOUL name / rename (46), Look ‹ › (60 × 52),
  the Maps rim buttons (52), Convert's rows (50), the ↑ disc in Settings › Apps (59), Control's tiles (58), walking
  nav (56). Each needs a new layout, not just bigger numbers.
- **Cut by the circle:** the Maps and Bridge headers (−15 / −5 px x), alarm ringing's Snooze, the Wi-Fi hotspot
  buttons, the boot "No AI" step, the alarm time picker's labels, Control's bottom tile.
- **The keyboard** (Nunito 26 keys, 44 px pitch = 4.1 mm on S). It needs an S keyboard: fewer keys per row, or a
  T9 / rim-letter picker plus word suggestions. Until the codecs are driven, typing is the only text input on S.
- **Rim collisions:** Weather's bottom hint touches a word.
- **Not audited, seen on the stills:** the dial's speech bubble (2 lines × 236 px) overlaps the tips of the ±1
  pebbles; a narrow lower neighbour truncates its title ("W… Acasa ›"); Alarms' sub-lines ("weekdays …").
- **The screens still on Nunito** (keyboard, time picker labels) and the app screens in `OsAppsDraw.cpp` and
  `OsMemory.cpp` use literal design px. Move them onto `dens_` tokens the same way, and keep M pixel-identical
  (`perf_bench.py --hashes` before/after, `--check`).
- **Perf on S** [estimare]: a full-screen push is 466 × 466 × 2 = 434 KB over QSPI (Arduino_GFX, ~40–80 MHz). A
  whole frame (the lid wipe, 290 ms) is then roughly 25–45 ms. Measure on the board (`F` serial command) and add an
  S column to PERF.md.
