## What S-P0 contains

S-P0 is the DIY pilot of SOUL S: the MĂRGĂRITAR S outline around the **stock Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75** with its stock Ø48.96 glass. CAD: `prototip/cad/soul_p0_s.py`; print files: `prototip/stl/s/` (with its assembly note), `prototip/step/s/`; drawing: `blueprints/final/soul_s_2_p0_kit.png`.
- **Body: 63.0 × 70.8 × 25.65 mm** (the uncompressed back). It is the only depth where the stock 8.7 mm board stack plus a cell fits (`12-SIZE-S.md` §2.3–2.4). The product is 23.0 mm, with its own PCB.
- **Parts:** front shell, back shell, a printed chassis, PWR/BOOT key pins.
- **Cell:** a 503030 class (≈ 450 mAh [E], ≈ 16 h [E]), as in `size-s.json` and the exported CAD. The 503035 (500 mAh) first planned does not fit beside the USB-C plug tunnel (`12-SIZE-S.md` §2.3).
- **Closure:** 4 × M2 from the back (2 × M2×12 lower, 2 × M2×8 upper).
- **Charging:** over the board's own USB-C. Pogo contacts and the OU dock are optional.

Variants in the CAD script: `plastic` (exported), `alu` and `alu_band` (CNC 6061, 1.4 mm walls; a 3 mm printed seam band as the RF window). Only the plastic variant is exported today.
