Taken from Starboy

  • Big, bold, flat. Two egg-shaped whites that nearly touch, solid colour on pure black, no glow and no blur.
  • Pupils do the acting. Big solid pupils that dart, with the second eye a beat behind the first.
  • Heterochromia as a collectible trait.
  • The blink. A flat lid drops while the eye squashes and sinks, closing to a thin bowl, then springs back.

Ours

  • The pebble glint. A crisp cream pebble on every pupil, the same shape as SOUL itself. It reads as black glass catching the light and works as a mark even at 40 px.
  • Șmecher attitude. A side-eye with one eye squinting under a tilted lid while the other stays wide open. It's the brand's personality as an expression.
  • AE timing everywhere. Every move has anticipation, overshoot and settle (squash before the surprise pop, pupils that shrink before hearts burst), plus little idle hops.
  • Warm cream hero. The hero whites are SOUL cream instead of neon, with saturated pupils, so it looks premium and stays on brand. The neon colourways become the rarer pulls.
